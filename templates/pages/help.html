SUPPORT & FAQ

How can we help?

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive an email with your tracking number. You can also view your order status by logging into your account and navigating to the "Orders" section in your Dashboard.

Account Issues

If you have forgotten your password, click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. An email will be sent to you with instructions on how to reset it securely.

Payment Methods

We currently accept all major Credit Cards, Debit Cards, and UPI (Unified Payments Interface). We also offer Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible regions.

Still need help?

Our concierge team is available to assist you. Please reach out to us at support@nexuskart.com or call us at +91 1234 567 890 during business hours.
